Dice and NSD are unitless in [0,1] (higher is better); HD95 is reported in millimeters (lower is better). Mean values follow the thesis's own aggregation convention: Dice Mean is pooled across foreground voxels, while HD95 Mean and NSD Mean are the arithmetic mean of the five sub-region scores. Per-epoch validation here runs without test-time augmentation (tta_flip_dims=()) to keep training-loop cost down. Sensitivity, Precision, and Specificity are plain voxel-wise confusion-matrix rates per class, averaged the same arithmetic way as HD95/NSD Mean.
