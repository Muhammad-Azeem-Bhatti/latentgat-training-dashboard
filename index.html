Latent Graph Attention for Glioma Segmentation

A 3D U-Net encoder–decoder with a k-NN graph attention layer (k=5, 4 heads) inserted at the bottleneck, treating the latent feature map as a graph so distant tumor sub-regions can attend to one another before decoding — the diagram on the right shows one node attending to its five nearest neighbors. Trained on BraTS2021 (1,251 cases, 251 held out for validation); the epoch-156 checkpoint was selected by combined Dice+HD95+NSD rank and validated for out-of-distribution generalization on two BraTS-Africa cohorts and a second glioma cohort, UCSF-PDGM.
