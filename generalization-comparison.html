Custom3DUNet_LatentGAT · out-of-distribution evaluation

BraTS2021 vs. BraTS-Africa

Same trained checkpoint (epoch 140, the best-scoring epoch on the combined Dice+HD95 rank), evaluated on three cohorts: the home validation split (BraTS2021, n=251), an out-of-distribution external glioma cohort (BraTS-Africa — Glioma, n=95), and a same-country cohort of non-glioma brain tumors (BraTS-Africa — Other Neoplasms, n=51) the model was never trained to segment. Same evaluate_full_metrics pipeline, same 4-view TTA, same post-processing settings for all three.

Dice and NSD are unitless in [0,1] (higher is better); HD95 is reported in millimeters (lower is better). "Mean" is the arithmetic mean of the five sub-region scores, matching the training dashboard's convention. Both BraTS-Africa cohorts' labels are public (TCIA-hosted); BraTS2021 numbers come from the tracked epoch-70 validation checkpoint, not a separate held-out submission. Other Neoplasms is a non-glioma cohort (meningiomas, metastases, and other CNS tumors) included as an out-of-task robustness check, not as evidence for or against glioma generalization.

Checkpoint-selection note: three checkpoints have now been tested externally (epochs 70, 90, 140), and the relationship between home-cohort rank and external generalization isn't monotonic. Epoch 90 — better than epoch 70 at home — generalized worse to both BraTS-Africa cohorts on every metric. Epoch 140 — the current home-cohort best, and better than epoch 90 at home too — recovers most of that external ground (Glioma Dice_Mean 0.791 vs. epoch 90's 0.781, back near epoch 70's 0.784; Other Neoplasms Dice_Mean 0.723 vs. epoch 90's 0.717, also the best of the three), though its Glioma HD95_Mean (18.6mm) still trails epoch 70's (17.7mm). Net read: home-cohort combined-rank selection correlates with but does not guarantee the best external generalization at every checkpoint — worth reporting as its own finding rather than assuming later epochs are strictly better all round.
