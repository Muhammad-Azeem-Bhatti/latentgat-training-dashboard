Custom3DUNet_LatentGAT · out-of-distribution evaluation

BraTS2021 vs. BraTS-Africa

Same trained checkpoint (epoch 156, the best epoch on the combined Dice+HD95 rank across all 200 epochs), evaluated on four cohorts: the home validation split (BraTS2021, n=251), an out-of-distribution external glioma cohort (BraTS-Africa — Glioma, n=95), and a cohort of non-glioma neoplasms from the same source (BraTS-Africa — Other Neoplasms, n=51) that the model was never trained to segment, and a second glioma cohort (UCSF-PDGM, n=74, patients that also appear in BraTS2021 removed). All four evaluations use identical inference settings (see the note below), so home and external numbers are directly comparable.

Dice and NSD are unitless in [0,1] (higher is better); HD95 is reported in millimeters (lower is better). "Mean" is the arithmetic mean of the five sub-region scores, matching the training dashboard's convention. Both BraTS-Africa cohorts' labels are public (TCIA-hosted); BraTS2021 numbers come from a dedicated evaluation of the epoch-156 checkpoint on the home validation split, not a separate held-out submission. Other Neoplasms is a non-glioma cohort included as an out-of-task robustness check, not as evidence for or against glioma generalization.

Inference settings (identical for all four cohorts): sliding-window overlap 0.625 with constant blending, 4-view test-time augmentation (identity plus one flip per axis), and no post-processing. For reference, the same checkpoint without TTA scores Dice_Mean 0.8654, HD95_Mean 9.12mm and NSD_Mean 0.8915 on the home split (the per-epoch training-validation pass); TTA adds about 0.003 Dice and removes about 0.45mm of HD95.

UCSF-PDGM note: plain (not bias-corrected) images were used; the bias-corrected images scored clearly worse on a 20-patient comparison (that choice was made on 20 of the 74 evaluated patients). Only baseline scans are included; the 53 patients found in BraTS2021 and all follow-up scans are excluded. These 74 are a contiguous block of later-enrolled patients, not a random sample of the cohort, and the download of the remaining patients is not yet complete, so the count and numbers will change. The UCSF bars in the charts use the same all-cases convention as the other cohorts; the asterisked values above and the table below the charts show the region-aware view.

Checkpoint-selection note: four checkpoints have now been tested externally (epochs 70, 90, 140, 156; compared here assuming the same evaluation settings across those runs), and home-cohort rank does not predict external generalization monotonically. Epoch 90, better than epoch 70 at home, generalized worse to both BraTS-Africa cohorts on every headline metric. Epoch 156, the selected checkpoint, has the best Glioma Dice_Mean (0.795 vs. 0.784 / 0.781 / 0.791 for epochs 70 / 90 / 140) and the best Glioma HD95_Mean (17.5mm vs. 17.7 / 18.9 / 18.6), but its Glioma NSD_Mean (0.773) sits slightly below epochs 70 and 140 (0.777, 0.775); on Other Neoplasms it ranks second on Dice and HD95, behind epoch 70. The differences between the top checkpoints (about 0.01 Dice, 1.5mm HD95) are small next to the per-case spread (Dice std about 0.16), so external results should be reported for the selected checkpoint rather than cherry-picked across epochs.
