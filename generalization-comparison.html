Custom3DUNet_LatentGAT · out-of-distribution evaluation

BraTS2021 vs. BraTS-Africa

Same trained checkpoint (epoch 70, the best-scoring epoch on the combined Dice+HD95 rank), evaluated on two populations: the home validation split (BraTS2021, n=251) and an out-of-distribution external cohort (BraTS-Africa, n=95). Same evaluate_full_metrics pipeline, same 4-view TTA, same post-processing settings for both runs.

Dice and NSD are unitless in [0,1] (higher is better); HD95 is reported in millimeters (lower is better). "Mean" is the arithmetic mean of the five sub-region scores, matching the training dashboard's convention. BraTS-Africa labels are public (TCIA-hosted); BraTS2021 numbers come from the tracked epoch-70 validation checkpoint, not a separate held-out submission.
