Custom3DUNet_LatentGAT · out-of-distribution evaluation

BraTS2021 vs. BraTS-Africa

Same trained checkpoint (epoch 156, the best epoch on the combined Dice+HD95 rank across all 200 epochs), evaluated on three cohorts: the home validation split (BraTS2021, n=251), an out-of-distribution external glioma cohort (BraTS-Africa — Glioma, n=95), and a cohort of non-glioma neoplasms from the same source (BraTS-Africa — Other Neoplasms, n=51) that the model was never trained to segment. See the comparability note below before comparing home and external numbers directly.

Dice and NSD are unitless in [0,1] (higher is better); HD95 is reported in millimeters (lower is better). "Mean" is the arithmetic mean of the five sub-region scores, matching the training dashboard's convention. Both BraTS-Africa cohorts' labels are public (TCIA-hosted); BraTS2021 numbers come from the epoch-156 per-epoch validation pass, not a separate held-out submission. Other Neoplasms is a non-glioma cohort included as an out-of-task robustness check, not as evidence for or against glioma generalization.

Comparability note: the home values are the per-epoch validation pass, which runs without test-time augmentation. The external runs use evaluate_full_metrics on the same checkpoint; if their TTA setting differs from the home pass, the home-vs-external gap is over- or understated by that difference.

Checkpoint-selection note: four checkpoints have now been tested externally (epochs 70, 90, 140, 156), and home-cohort rank does not predict external generalization monotonically. Epoch 90, better than epoch 70 at home, generalized worse to both BraTS-Africa cohorts on every headline metric. Epoch 156, the selected checkpoint, has the best Glioma Dice_Mean (0.795 vs. 0.784 / 0.781 / 0.791 for epochs 70 / 90 / 140) and the best Glioma HD95_Mean (17.5mm vs. 17.7 / 18.9 / 18.6), but its Glioma NSD_Mean (0.773) sits slightly below epochs 70 and 140 (0.777, 0.775); on Other Neoplasms it ranks second on Dice and HD95, behind epoch 70. The differences between the top checkpoints (about 0.01 Dice, 1.5mm HD95) are small next to the per-case spread (Dice std about 0.16), so external results should be reported for the selected checkpoint rather than cherry-picked across epochs.
